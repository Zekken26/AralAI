# AralAI User Personas

These personas represent the initial users of the AralAI MVP. They are working assumptions and should be updated after real student and teacher interviews.

# 1. Student Persona

## Basic Information

**Name:** Juan Dela Cruz
**Age:** 14
**Grade Level:** Grade 8
**Location:** Biliran Province, Philippines
**Primary Device:** Android mobile phone
**Internet Access:** Prepaid mobile data or shared Wi-Fi
**Technology Skill Level:** Basic to intermediate

## Background

Juan attends a public high school and studies Mathematics as part of his Grade 8 curriculum. He can follow examples during class, but he sometimes struggles when solving similar problems independently.

He usually studies using notes, videos, group chats, and internet searches. He has limited access to private tutoring and may hesitate to ask questions during class because he is afraid of giving a wrong answer.

## Goals

* Pass Grade 8 Mathematics.
* Understand Algebra and word problems.
* Prepare for quizzes and examinations.
* Improve confidence when solving problems.
* Know which topics require more practice.
* Receive explanations that are easy to understand.

## Pain Points

* Long explanations are difficult to follow.
* He forgets lesson procedures after class.
* Search results may use unfamiliar methods.
* General AI tools may provide answers without teaching the process.
* He does not always know why his answer is wrong.
* Slow internet makes video-based learning difficult.
* He may only study immediately before an examination.

## Needs

* Mobile-friendly lessons.
* Short and clear explanations.
* Worked examples.
* Progressive hints.
* Practice questions with immediate feedback.
* A visible record of completed and weak topics.
* Low-bandwidth access.
* Encouragement without embarrassment.

## Typical Behaviors

* Uses a mobile phone more often than a computer.
* Studies after school or before examinations.
* Prefers examples before definitions.
* Repeats questions when the first explanation is confusing.
* Stops practicing when tasks become too difficult.
* Responds well to short progress indicators.

## User Quote

> I understand the example in class, but I get confused when I try a different problem at home.

## User Stories

* As a student, I want to view lessons by topic so that I can review difficult subjects.
* As a student, I want to receive hints so that I can solve the problem myself.
* As a student, I want to review incorrect answers so that I understand my mistakes.
* As a student, I want to see my weak topics so that I know what to study next.
* As a student, I want the platform to work on my phone so that I can study anywhere.

# 2. Teacher Persona

## Basic Information

**Name:** Maria Santos
**Age:** 34
**Role:** Grade 8 Mathematics Teacher
**Teaching Experience:** 10 years
**School Type:** Public high school
**Primary Device:** Laptop and Android phone
**Technology Skill Level:** Intermediate

## Background

Maria handles several Grade 8 classes with many students. She prepares lesson plans, quizzes, assignments, and student records while managing limited classroom time.

She wants to give additional practice to struggling students but cannot always provide individual explanations. She currently uses printed activities, presentation slides, messaging applications, and spreadsheets.

## Goals

* Improve student understanding and performance.
* Identify weak topics before examinations.
* Reduce time spent preparing repetitive quizzes.
* Monitor student participation and completion.
* Provide additional practice based on student needs.
* Maintain control over published learning content.

## Pain Points

* Preparing different question sets takes significant time.
* Manual checking delays feedback.
* It is difficult to identify learning gaps across large classes.
* Students may copy answers from online tools.
* Existing dashboards may show scores without explaining weaknesses.
* AI-generated content may contain incorrect answers.
* Managing several tools creates additional work.

## Needs

* Simple classroom and enrollment management.
* A lesson and quiz editor.
* Automatic scoring.
* Topic-level analytics.
* A list of students who need support.
* Teacher approval before AI-generated content is published.
* Exportable assessment results.
* Clear audit records for important changes.

## Typical Behaviors

* Prepares materials outside class hours.
* Reuses and modifies previous quiz questions.
* Reviews class averages after assessments.
* Communicates through messaging applications.
* Prefers tools that require minimal training.
* Will stop using a system if it adds duplicate administrative work.

## User Quote

> I need to know which students are struggling before the examination, not after they fail it.

## User Stories

* As a teacher, I want to create a classroom so that I can organize my students.
* As a teacher, I want to publish lessons and quizzes so that students can access them.
* As a teacher, I want quizzes to be scored automatically so that students receive faster feedback.
* As a teacher, I want to see topic-level results so that I can adjust my teaching.
* As a teacher, I want to review AI-generated questions before publishing them so that I can verify their accuracy.

# 3. Persona-Based Design Requirements

The product should:

* Use a mobile-first student interface.
* Support readable mathematical notation.
* Minimize the number of steps needed to start a lesson.
* Provide text-based content that works on slow connections.
* Avoid displaying complex analytics to students.
* Provide actionable analytics to teachers.
* Require teacher review for generated educational content.
* Explain why a lesson or exercise is recommended.
* Use plain language appropriate for Grade 8 students.
* Avoid unnecessary collection of personal data.
